# Hi there, I'm Jaiany! 👋

I am a **PhD Candidate at UFRGS** and a **Visiting Fellow at Harvard University**, specializing in **Marketing Analytics, Bayesian Statistical Modeling, and Geospatial Data Science**. My work heavily leverages Bayesian inference, machine learning, and spatial econometrics to solve complex, high-dimensional business problems, such as market entry viability, demand estimation, and consumer behavior analysis.

- 🔭 **I’m currently working on:** Scaling advanced Bayesian spatial architectures and predictive modeling frameworks to evaluate geo-marketing viability and high-dimensional demand forecasting.
- 🌱 **I’m currently learning:** High-Performance Computing (`HPC`) clusters for large-scale spatial simulations and expanding my toolkit in cloud-based data science pipelines.

---

### 🧠 Main Methodological Focus

My research and technical workflow bridge advanced statistical frameworks and predictive modeling, prioritizing structural inference, uncertainty quantification, and high-resolution spatial mapping. I focus heavily on:
- **Bayesian & Spatial Analysis:** Modeling complex spatial dependencies and geographic variances using advanced Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) and integrated nested Laplace approximations.
- **Machine Learning Integration:** Combining the predictive power of machine learning algorithms (`TensorFlow`/`Scikit-Learn`) with hierarchical statistical models to uncover hidden data structures.
- **Model Validation:** Evaluating spatial architectures using rigorous out-of-sample predictive checks, information criteria, and robust predictive metrics (such as PSIS-LOO).
